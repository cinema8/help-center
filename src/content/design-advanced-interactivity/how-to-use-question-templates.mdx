# How to Use Question Templates

## Give your questions your own look

A **question template** draws the questions of your videos in a look of its own: a quiz card in your brand colours, a five-star rating, picture cards to tap, a worksheet with words to drag. You write the questions in Creative Studio as usual (the text, the options, the right answer, the feedback, what happens next); the template only decides how they look and how viewers answer. One template draws every question that uses it, in every video, each with its own words.

A template draws the question types it lists: single choice, multiple choice, free text, fill in the blanks, drag and drop, matching. A question of a type it does not draw keeps the player's own look, and true/false and recorded (audio and video) answers are always drawn by the player.

<Steps>

### Step 1 — Open Customisation

In the main menu, click **Customisation**, then the **Question templates** tab. Customisation has three tabs: **Player themes**, **Widgets** and **Question templates**.

![The Question templates tab of Customisation](/screenshot/widgets-v3/question-templates-list.webp)

The list shows your templates with the question types each one draws, whether it is published, and how many videos use it. **Used in** opens the list of videos and player themes that use it.

### Step 2 — Make a template

Click **New question template**. There are three ways to start:

- **Describe it, and AI Writer writes it.** Write how the questions should look, how viewers answer and what they see after the answer, or pick one of the suggestions (a quiz for every question type, a five-star rating, picture cards, a word bank, a minimal card, your brand colours). AI Writer uses your account's AI tokens; the window shows the cost before you start.
- **Start from** a ready template: **Quiz card** (a dark card in the middle of the video) or **Side panel** (a light panel beside the video, which stays in sight). Both draw every question type; you get your own copy to change.
- **Blank**: empty code with the question types you choose, for developers.

**Duplicate a question template…** copies one of yours or one from the Cinema8 library, and **Import code…** brings in a template exported from another account.

![The New question template window](/screenshot/widgets-v3/new-question-template.webp)

### Step 3 — Try it in the editor

The editor shows your template drawing a sample question on a sample video, at desktop, tablet and phone sizes. The switch above the video (**Single**, **Multiple**, **Free text**, **Blanks**, **Drag & drop**, **Matching**) shows the sample in each type.

![A question template in the editor](/screenshot/widgets-v3/question-template-editor.webp)

The **Question** tab on the right holds:

- **Draws these question types:** tick the types the template should draw. Each one is checked as you work: **Drawn**, or what is missing.
- **Sample question:** change the sample to see how the template copes: more options, pictures in the options, long texts, a question viewers can skip, an answer on click, a hidden question text.
- **Result screen (preview):** answer the sample as right, wrong or not graded, with feedback, to see what viewers see after they answer. Nothing is sent anywhere from the editor.

The **About** tab holds the template's name, description and picture, shown when creators choose a template. To change the template, use **Write with AI** in the top bar (describe the change, then review it before it goes in) or open **Code** to edit the HTML, CSS and JavaScript yourself. Your changes are saved as you work, as a draft: videos keep the published version until you publish.

### Step 4 — Publish

Click **Publish**. The window says how many questions, videos and player themes change at once, lists anything the editor's checks found, and asks you to confirm if you stopped drawing a type that questions use (those questions then go back to the player's own look). Each publish is a new version; **Version history** in the ⋯ menu can bring an earlier one back.

### Step 5 — Apply it to your questions

There are two ways to use a template:

1. **For one question**, in Creative Studio: select the question, open its **Appearance** page, choose **Template** under **Drawn with**, then pick the template. Pointing at a template previews it on the video.
2. **For every question of a player theme**: **Customisation → Player themes**, open the theme, and choose it as its **Question template**. Every video with that theme draws its questions with the template, unless a question picks another one on its Appearance page.

![The Appearance page of a question in Creative Studio](/screenshot/widgets-v3/appearance-page.webp)

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  A template chosen in a **player theme** applies to every question of the videos using that theme, while a template chosen on a question's **Appearance** page affects only that question.
</Callout>

<Callout type="default">
  **For developers:** question templates are written with Widget API v2. The question's fields, the answer for each type and a complete example are on [Question templates with Widget API v2](/advanced-tools-for-developers/question-template-api).
</Callout>
