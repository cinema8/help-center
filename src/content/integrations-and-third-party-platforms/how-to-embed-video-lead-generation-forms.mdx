# How to Embed In-Video Lead Generation Forms in Cinema8

Cinema8 allows you to create interactive videos with built-in lead capture forms and connect them directly to platforms like **Mailchimp**, **HubSpot**, **Constant Contact** and **Campaign Monitor**.

With Cinema8, you can embed video lead generation forms directly inside your videos, collect viewer information without sending users to external landing pages, and automatically sync leads into your CRM or email marketing workflows.

This guide explains how to set up in-video lead generation, connect form integrations, and embed lead generation forms inside interactive videos.

![CRM Integration](https://static-01.cinema8.com/gallery/28354/0c81ddd8-0805-43a7-b376-2a97fde47d21/crmintegration_2-min-min.webp)

## What is in-video lead generation?

In-video lead generation is the process of collecting viewer information directly inside a video experience using embedded forms, CTA buttons, booking widgets, or interactive overlays.

Instead of redirecting viewers to another page, Cinema8 allows users to:

- Capture leads inside the video player
- Embed lead generation forms into videos
- Sync viewer data with CRM and email marketing platforms
- Increase conversion rates with interactive video experiences

Cinema8 supports:

- Video lead generation form embedding
- Interactive lead capture forms
- CRM-integrated video forms
- In-video email signup forms
- Interactive video CTAs
- Video-based customer qualification workflows

## Supported integrations for video lead generation

Cinema8 supports integrations with:

- **Mailchimp**
- **HubSpot**
- **Constant Contact**
- **Campaign Monitor**

These integrations help you automate video lead generation workflows and send collected viewer data directly into your marketing systems.

## How to connect a form integration in Cinema8

<Steps>
### Step 1: Open the integrations panel

- Go to the Cinema8 main menu
- Click **Integrations**
- Select one of the available integrations:
  - Mailchimp
  - HubSpot
  - Constant Contact
  - Campaign Monitor

### Step 2: Connect your account

- Click **Connect**
- Log into your selected platform
- Authorise Cinema8 access permissions
- Your integration will appear in the integrations panel once connected

### Step 3: Select a list or audience

Choose where collected leads should be stored.

Examples:

- Mailchimp audience list
- HubSpot CRM pipeline
- Constant Contact mailing list
- Campaign Monitor subscriber segment

### Step 4: Configure field mappings

Map Cinema8 form fields to your CRM or email marketing platform fields.

Common mappings include:

- Email address
- First name
- Last name
- Company name
- Phone number
- Job title

Example:

- Cinema8 Email → HubSpot Email
- Cinema8 Company → HubSpot Company Name

### Step 5: Save integration settings

- Review your setup
- Click **Save Settings**
</Steps>

## How to embed a video lead generation form in Cinema8

<Steps>
### Step 1: Create or open an interactive video project

- Open an existing project
- Or create a new interactive video

### Step 2: Add a lead generation form

- Open the **Components** panel
- Drag and drop the **Form** component onto the timeline
- Position the form at the desired point in the video

This allows you to embed an in-video lead generation form directly into your interactive content.

### Step 3: Configure your video lead capture form

Add the form fields you want viewers to complete.

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Email address
- Company
- Phone number
- Job title

You can also:

- Make fields required
- Add placeholder text
- Include GDPR consent checkboxes
- Customise CTA button labels
- Add validation rules

<Callout type="info">
Keep video lead generation forms short to improve conversion rates.
</Callout>

### Step 4: Connect the form to your CRM or email platform

Inside the form settings:

- Open the **Integration** section
- Select your connected platform
- Choose the destination audience or contact list
- Match form fields with CRM fields

Supported workflows include:

- Video-to-CRM lead capture
- Video email signup automation
- Interactive video contact collection
- Marketing automation triggers

### Step 5: Configure post-submit actions

After a viewer submits the form, Cinema8 can:

- Continue the video automatically
- Display a thank-you message
- Redirect users to another page
- Launch a Calendly booking widget
- Trigger another interactive element
- Show personalised video content

This helps turn interactive videos into conversion-focused lead generation experiences.

### Step 6: Preview and publish your interactive video

- Click **Save**
- Preview the full viewer experience
- Publish your video

Your in-video lead generation form is now live.
</Steps>

## Best practices for video lead generation

### Place forms after value-driven moments

The best time to show a lead generation form is after:

- Product demonstrations
- Case studies
- Educational content
- Pricing explanations
- ROI examples

This improves form completion rates and lead quality.

### Use clear CTA buttons

Examples:

- Book a demo
- Get the guide
- Request pricing
- Start free trial
- Speak to sales

Strong CTAs improve interactive video conversion rates.

### Optimise forms for mobile viewers

Many users watch videos on mobile devices. For better mobile UX:

- Use fewer fields
- Keep forms visually simple
- Avoid long text inputs
- Use large CTA buttons

### Monitor video lead generation performance

Track metrics such as:

- Form completion rate
- Viewer drop-off rate
- CTA click-through rate
- Video retention
- Lead conversion rate

This helps optimise your interactive video funnel over time.
