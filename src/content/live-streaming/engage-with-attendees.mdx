# How do I engage with attendees during the live stream?

If you're broadcasting through a Cinema8 watch page, you can use Comments, Q&A, and polls to engage with attendees while your event is live.

## Use Comments for live conversation

Comments let attendees send messages while watching your live stream.

When an attendee sends something for the first time, Cinema8 asks them to provide a name. They do not need to create a Cinema8 account.

## Manage attendee questions with Q&A

Use Q&A when you want attendees to submit questions separately from the live conversation.

Submitted questions can be moderated before they are shown to the audience. From the Q&A controls, you can:

- Review pending questions.
- Approve questions.
- Answer questions.
- Pin a question.
- Hide a question.
- Delete a question.

When you answer a question, the answer appears underneath the question on the watch page.

## Run polls during the live stream

Polls let you ask attendees questions and collect responses during the broadcast.

You can:

- Allow attendees to select more than one answer.
- Set a timer to close a poll automatically.
- Prepare a poll as a draft or open it immediately.
- View results while the poll is open.

Attendees can see live results and the countdown when a timer is being used.

## See how many attendees are watching

The Cinema8 watch page displays the current number of viewers next to the **LIVE** indicator.

This count reflects viewers watching through the Cinema8 watch page as well as viewers watching your broadcast through external destinations such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and Facebook.

## What happens next?

When the live stream ends, you can manage the recording, replay, and other post-event content.
