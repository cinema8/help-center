# How do I track my live stream performance?

After your live stream ends, you can use the **Analytics** tab to review audience and engagement data from your event. Cinema8 shows viewing metrics alongside activity from features such as Comments, polls, and Q&A.

## Open your live stream analytics

Open the live event you want to review and select the **Analytics** tab.

At the top of the page, you can see a summary of your event performance, including:

- **Peak viewers:** The peak viewer count recorded for your live event.
- **Duration:** The duration of your live broadcast.
- **Avg watch (Cinema8):** The average amount of time viewers watched through Cinema8.
- **Chat msg:** The number of Comments sent during the event.
- **Poll votes:** The total number of votes submitted through polls.

## View audience activity over time

The **Viewers over time** chart shows how your viewer count changed throughout the live stream.

Use the timeline to see how your audience changed at different points during the broadcast.

## Review poll results

If you launched polls during your live stream, the **Polls** section shows how your audience responded.

For each poll, you can see:

- The total number of votes.
- The number of votes for each answer.
- The percentage of votes received by each answer.

Polls are part of the Cinema8 watch-page experience and are available on [**Live Scale and Live Enterprise plans**](https://cinema8.com/live-streaming-pricing).

## Review Q&A activity

The **Q&A** section shows the questions submitted during your live stream.

You can see how many questions were:

- Submitted.
- Pending.
- Approved.
- Answered.

You can also review individual questions and answers, along with the number of upvotes received.

Q&A is also available through the Cinema8 watch page on **Live Scale and Live Enterprise plans**.

## View your replay and VOD analytics

If recording was enabled and your recording has finished processing, a **Replay available** section appears in the event analytics.

From here, you can:

- Select **Watch replay** to open the recorded broadcast.
- Select **VOD analytics** to view analytics for the recorded video.
- Select **Open video details** to open the recording in Cinema8.

The recording is also added to your Cinema8 video library as an editable video. The replay uses the same watch-page link that viewers used for the live event.

## What happens next?

Once you've reviewed your live stream performance, you can use the event analytics to understand how viewers watched and interacted with your broadcast.
